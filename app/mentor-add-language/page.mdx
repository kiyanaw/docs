# Contribute to kiyânaw Mentor

## Add Your Language or Dialect
New languages, dialects and communities can be added to kiyânaw Mentor easily. [Contact us](https://bundle.kiyanaw.net/support) to get started.

## Become a Mentor
Are you a language mentor for your community? [Contact us](https://bundle.kiyanaw.net/support) to update your account and get you started contributing content.
