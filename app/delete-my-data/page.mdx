# Delete My Data

**This page applies to the following apps and services, all operated by
Cloudcraft Software Inc.:**

- **kiyânaw Transcribe** web app
- **kiyânaw Mentor** mobile app (`net.kiyanaw.mentor`)
- **Our Métis Languages** mobile app (`net.kiyanaw.omg`)

This page explains how to permanently delete your account and the personal data
associated with it across the kiyânaw platform.

## How to request deletion

Email **support@kiyanaw.net** from the email address associated with your account
(or include your account details in the message) with the subject
**"Delete My Data"**. We use your account email to verify the request.

## What gets deleted

When we process your request, we permanently delete your account and **all** personal
data associated with it.

## Data retention

We permanently delete your account and all associated data **within 30 days** of your
request. We may retain a limited subset of data only where required for legal,
security, or fraud-prevention purposes, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
