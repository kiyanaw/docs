## Our Guiding Principles

kiyânaw exists to support Indigenous Language Revitalization. Our work is guided by **kindness**, **truth**, **strength**, and **sharing**, and informed by the principles of Indigenous Data Sovereignty — including the CARE Principles (Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, Ethics).

We act as **stewards** of this platform, not owners of what it holds. Where these Terms are ambiguous, we will interpret them in favour of the contributing individuals and communities whose languages are represented here.

## Acceptable Use

When using kiyânaw, please:

- Only contribute content you have the right to share. If a recording features another person's voice or another community's knowledge, make sure you have their consent.
- Respect community protocols for sacred, ceremonial, or otherwise restricted material. When in doubt, don't post it.
- Treat other users — learners, mentors, elders — with respect.

When using kiyânaw, please **do not**:

- Scrape, bulk-download, mirror, or otherwise harvest content from the platform or the Language Database. Database access is actively monitored and misuse will result in an immediate ban.
- Use kiyânaw content to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or otherwise build artificial intelligence or machine-learning systems. See the AI section below.
- Resell, sublicense, or commercialize access to kiyânaw or its content (other than under a separate written agreement with Cloudcraft Software Inc. — see *Institutional and Organizational Licensing* below).
- Attempt to access accounts, content, or systems you have not been granted access to.
- Use the platform to harass, defame, or harm others, or to upload unlawful material.

## Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

kiyânaw content — including audio, transcriptions, translations, and database entries — **may not be used to train, fine-tune, evaluate, retrieve-augment, or otherwise inform any AI or machine-learning system** without the explicit, prior, written consent of both the relevant community and every contributing individual involved.

This restriction applies to commercial AI providers, academic researchers, hobbyists, automated agents, and institutional licensees alike. It applies whether the content is accessed through normal app use, the Language Database, or any other surface.

We take this position because the use of Indigenous language data in AI systems raises unresolved questions about consent, ownership, and cultural harm. Until communities themselves determine if, when, and how AI should interact with their languages, our default is *no*.

- **All community content is opted out of organizational licensing by default.** No content from any community will be surfaced to an institutional licensee unless that community has been consulted and has confirmed which materials they are comfortable making available.

**How we think about the money.** Our financial interest is straightforward: we want kiyânaw to be sustainable. The primary purpose of any institutional licensing revenue is to cover the **operating and development costs** of keeping the platform running and improving for everyone. If we are ever fortunate enough for revenue to exceed those costs, we will seek the **guidance of the community** on how those funds should be directed.

If you have input on how institutional licensing should work, or on how any surplus funds should be used, we'd love for you to join our next **community engagement session** — this is exactly the kind of question we want to discuss together.
